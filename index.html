CivicFix

A full-stack platform where people report civic problems and follow them until they’re fixed.

Role-based dashboards for residents and admins, JWT login, and a full complaint lifecycle. Admins can monitor, filter and update status. A rule-based classifier assigns each complaint a category, priority and the right civic department.

Customer Segmentation Platform

Sorts e-commerce customers into groups so a business knows who to focus on.

Customers are scored on recency, frequency and monetary value (RFM), grouped with K-means clustering, and presented in Power BI dashboards with insights a business can act on.

  • Python
  • SQL
  • K-means
  • Power BI
